🌿 MeloFlow — Guided Brain Dump & Triage

Externalize the mental noise. Pick one thing. Be kind to your brain.

What to do here: Tap how your brain feels right now. Matching your task size to your actual fuel stops procrastination before it starts.
🧠 2. Mental Tab Close (Brain Dump)
What to do here: Type every task, random thought, worry, or side-quest rattling around in your head and press Enter. Don't organize yet—just get it out of your RAM.

    What to do here: Click the 👑 crown on any task below to make it your single main win for today. Break it into a tiny 2-minute starter step so your brain doesn't freeze.
    ⚡ 5-Min Dopamine Wins
    What to do here: Keep tiny, low-effort tasks here (replying to one text, clearing one dish). Knock one out whenever you feel stuck to build quick momentum.
