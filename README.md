# 🌿 MeloFlow — Guided ADHD Brain Dump & Triage

A calm, zero-shame daily brain-dump and task triage widget designed to clear mental clutter, break through task initiation freeze, and focus on momentum over perfection.

## ✨ What It Does
* **Mental Battery Check-In:** Match your task size to your actual energy level (*Low/Foggy*, *Steady*, *Charged*, or *Hyperfocus*).
* **Mental Tab Close (Brain Dump):** Quickly offload tasks, worries, and random thoughts out of your head.
* **The Rule of One:** Isolate your single main win for the day with a **2-Minute Micro-Step** box and a gentle countdown timer (`5m`, `15m`, `25m`).
* **5-Min Dopamine Wins & Parking Lot:** Separate quick momentum builders from shiny side-quests (*Later, Not Never*).
* **Reverse To-Do List:** Automatically tracks everything you finish alongside one-tap self-care wins (*Drank water*, *Ate food*, *Took meds*, *Rested*).

## 🔒 Privacy & Saving Your List
* **100% Private:** Everything you type is saved locally right in your own web browser (`localStorage`). Nothing is sent to a server or visible to anyone else.
* **Tip for Phones:** If you open this link from a text message, Instagram, or Messenger, tap **`⋮` (or the Safari/Compass icon) → Open in Chrome / Open in Safari** first, then tap **Add to Home Screen**. Opening it inside a messaging app's mini-browser may erase your list when you close the window.
